    Under the Sea

    DOWN UNDER, DOWN UNDER: DIVING ADVENTURES ON THE GREAT BARRIER REEF

    A 12-year-old girl scuba dives in Australia’s Great Barrier Reef and meets one-horned unicorn fish, adorable clown fish, giant clams and more. One day she is surrounded by deadly sea snakes, ten feet long!

    NIGHT DIVE

    A 12-year-old girl is scared to death, about to do her first night dive. But what she finds beneath the sea and her adventures give her new memories she can never forget. Her underwater light fails, she is separated from her dive partner, and encounters a moray eel.

    THE UNDERWATER WORLD OF THE CORAL REEF

    In this enchanting book, the creatures of the tropical seas reveal their secrets and mysteries from dawn to dusk and dark night. Science, poetry and dazzling full-color photographs merge to create a magical undersea world.

    THE DESERT BENEATH THE SEA

    McGovern teams up with Dr. Eugenie Clark (The Shark Lady) to scuba dive in the Caribbean and tells about the strange creatures that inhabit the shallow, sandy-bottomed “desert.”

    QUESTIONS AND ANSWERS ABOUT SHARKS

    Everything you ever wanted to know about sharks — and were afraid to ask! This lively Q&A format makes shark facts accessible and exciting for young readers.

    SHARKS

    An exciting exploration of the world’s most feared ocean predators. Full of fascinating facts about shark biology, behavior and the importance of sharks in ocean ecosystems.
