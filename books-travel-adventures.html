    SWIMMING WITH SEA LIONS AND OTHER ADVENTURES IN THE GALAPAGOS ISLANDS

    Dolphins aglow with silver, playful sea lion pups, hammerhead sharks — all the wild creatures of these islands in South America. In her diary, a 12-year old girl describes her exciting adventures.

    PLAYING WITH PENGUINS AND OTHER ADVENTURES IN ANTARCTICA

    Penguins waddling in Antarctica, seals and glaciers. A 12-year-old girl writes a diary about her once-in-a-lifetime trip to Antarctica with her family and a team of scientists.
