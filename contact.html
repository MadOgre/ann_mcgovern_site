Classroom Resources

Teachers and librarians may request the following resources by using the contact form above:

  • Reading and discussion guides for individual books
  • Vocabulary and comprehension activities
  • Cross-curricular lesson plans linking Ann's books to history, science, and language arts standards
  • Information about bulk book orders for classroom use

Media Inquiries

For permissions, reprints, interviews, or other media inquiries, please contact the publisher directly or use the form above indicating "Media Inquiry" in the subject line. We will respond as quickly as possible.

Publishers & Rights Inquiries Rights and permissions for Ann McGovern's books are managed through the respective publishers. Please contact the publisher of the specific title you are inquiring about. A list of publishers can be found on the Books page.
