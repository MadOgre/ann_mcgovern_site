Welcome

Welcome to the official website of Ann McGovern, beloved children's book author and intrepid adventurer. Ann wrote more than 50 books for children over her remarkable career, spanning picture books, historical fiction, and nonfiction nature writing.

From the bustling farmyard of Too Much Noise to the deep blue world of coral reefs and sharks, Ann's books have captivated young readers for generations. Her passion for storytelling was matched only by her love of exploration — she was an avid scuba diver who traveled the world's oceans and brought those adventures to life on the page.

Explore this site to discover Ann's books, read about her extraordinary life, and learn about her school visit programs.

"I write for children because I have never forgotten what it feels like to be young, curious, and full of wonder about the world." — Ann McGovern

News & Updates

2018

Ann McGovern passed away on October 6, 2018, at age 88. She is remembered for her extraordinary body of work and the joy she brought to generations of young readers. Her books continue to be celebrated in schools and libraries around the world.

Legacy

Too Much Noise remains one of the most beloved picture books for early readers. The folktale-inspired story of a farmer who learns to appreciate the quiet of his own home has delighted children for over fifty years.
