    Animals

    ELEPHANT BABY: The Story of Little Tembo

    In Africa, Tembo lives with her family of nine elephants. Learn how she senses danger at an early age, how her herd finds food and water, why she does what other elephants do, and how she grows up to be an African elephant.

    LITTLE WHALE

    Follow a humpback whale from birth, through growing up, through the whale’s epic migrations and encounters with the deep ocean world. A powerful story of one of the ocean’s most magnificent creatures.

    GREAT GORILLAS

    Discover these mysterious, magnificent animals. From the forests of Africa to zoos around the world, Great Gorillas tells the fascinating story of this endangered great ape and its struggle to survive.
