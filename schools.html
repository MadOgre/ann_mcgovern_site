School Visits

Ann McGovern was a passionate advocate for literacy and loved visiting schools to share her love of books, history, and ocean adventure with young readers. Over the course of her career she visited hundreds of schools, speaking to thousands of students about the craft of writing and the joy of discovery.

Note for Educators

Ann McGovern passed away in October 2018. While she is no longer available for in-person visits, this page is preserved as a resource for educators who wish to use her books in the classroom. Archived materials, discussion guides, and activity sheets for many of her titles are available on request through the contact form.

Ann's School Programs

Ann offered a variety of programs tailored to different age groups and curriculum themes. Each program combined storytelling, Q&A, and interactive activities that brought her books to life in the classroom.

Program Grade Level Duration Description
The Magic of Folktales K–2 45 min An exploration of folktales and fables through Too Much Noise and Stone Soup, with interactive storytelling and discussion.
Living History 3–5 60 min Bringing history to life using the "If You Lived..." series. Students explore what life was really like in different historical periods.
Remarkable Women 4–6 60 min The stories of Harriet Tubman, Deborah Sampson, and Eugenie Clark — women who defied the odds. Connecting biography to research skills.
Beneath the Waves 3–8 60 min Exploring the ocean world through Ann's underwater adventures. Covers coral reefs, sharks, and ocean conservation. Includes slide presentation.
How to Write a Book 4–8 60–90 min A behind-the-scenes look at the writing process: research, drafts, revision, and publication. Inspires students to pursue their own writing.
Author's Assembly K–8 45–60 min A school-wide assembly suitable for all ages, combining storytelling, Q&A, and a celebration of books and reading.

Booking Information

What a Visit Includes

  • One or more classroom presentations or whole-school assembly
  • Q&A session with students
  • Book signing opportunity
  • Pre-visit materials for teachers (optional)

Preparing Your Students

To get the most out of an author visit, Ann recommended that students read at least one of her books beforehand and come prepared with questions. Activity guides and reading suggestions are available on request.
