    Picture Books

    STONE SOUP

    When a hungry young man is refused a meal from a greedy old woman, he asks her for a stone. “I can make soup from a stone,” he says. A classic folktale for young readers.

    TOO MUCH NOISE

    Peter complains that his house is too noisy, so the wise man advises him to get animals. How he solves his problem will amaze you.

    THE LADY IN THE BOX

    It is wintertime in the city. Ben and Lizzie want to help the lady who lives in a box instead of a home. Christmas is coming and it’s freezing cold. A warm-hearted story about homelessness for young readers.

    LITTLE WOLF

    Little Wolf is an Indian boy who will not hunt and shames his father. He brings home injured animals. When the chief’s son falls ill, Little Wolf becomes a hero.

    WHO HAS A SECRET?

    This book reveals some of the very best secrets so that the youngest child can share their mystery. Illustrated by Nola Langner.

    SQUEALS & SQUIGGLES & GHOSTLY GIGGLES

    A collection of silly and spooky tales including the popular story “The Velvet Ribbon.”

    NICHOLAS BENTLEY STONINGPOT III

    Nicholas is a dreadfully rude little boy. He is sure that the world revolves around him. Until a very special birthday wish teaches him an unforgettable lesson.

    HAPPY SILLY BIRTHDAY TO ME

    A silly, rhyming birthday story with a surprise ending. Perfect for the youngest readers.

    FEELING MAD, FEELING SAD, FEELING BAD, FEELING GLAD

    A simple, honest look at the spectrum of emotions children experience, told with warmth and humor.

    ZOO, WHERE ARE YOU?

    A delightful story about a child who discovers a whole zoo has somehow appeared in her house! Perfect for the youngest readers.

    EGGS ON YOUR NOSE

    A humorous story about a very messy eater! Kids will love seeing themselves in this relatable tale.
