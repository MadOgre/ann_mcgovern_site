    Currently Available

    STONE SOUP

    When a hungry young man is refused a meal from a greedy old woman, he asks her for a stone. “I can make soup from a stone,” he says. This beloved folktale has delighted children for generations.

    TOO MUCH NOISE

    Peter complains that his house is too noisy, so the wise man advises him to get animals who moo, hee-haw, cluck and baa. How he solves his problem will amaze you. This book has been delighting young readers for over 50 years.

    THE PILGRIMS’ FIRST THANKSGIVING

    The pilgrims’ first Thanksgiving lasted three whole days. This book tells you about the pilgrims’ first hard year at Plymouth Colony and the fascinating events that led to the celebration of a harvest feast.

    LITTLE WOLF

    Little Wolf is an Indian boy who will not hunt and shames his father. He brings home injured animals and birds and makes them well again. When the chief’s son falls ill, Little Wolf becomes a hero.

    WHO HAS A SECRET?

    There is nothing more pleasing than discovering a secret. This book reveals some of the very best secrets in such a way that the youngest child can share their mystery.

    SQUEALS & SQUIGGLES & GHOSTLY GIGGLES

    A wonderful collection including the popular story, “The Velvet Ribbon.” These silly and spooky tales are perfect for reading aloud at bedtime or Halloween.

    WHY IT’S A HOLIDAY

    Ann’s very first real book! The stories and origins behind the holidays we celebrate throughout the year, told in Ann’s engaging, easy-to-read style.
