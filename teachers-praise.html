    Praise for School Visits

    “Your presence at our annual ‘I Am An Author’ celebration made the event an affair to remember. You played an integral role in every aspect of the program, from autographing to sharing wonderful personal visions of your works. The audience remained spellbound as you showed your pictures and told the stories behind the books. I continue to receive accolades from educators, parents, and the community. On behalf of the Title 1 Reading Program of the Buffalo Public Schools, staff, students and parents, I wish to thank you for your deep commitment to our children.”
    — Allison Turley, Director of Federal Programs

    “You could certainly tell by the reaction of the children what a fine presentation you delivered. Thank you so much for helping to expand the lives of our students and their world.”
    — Scott Newland, Director, American International School of Lesotho

    “Many of your wonderful books have been loved and savored to the point that they are falling apart. Thank you for being such a source of joy and inspiration to young readers and writers.”
    — Mary Mahoney, Tanglewood School Library

    “The children were in awe during the assembly. Teachers have been raving about you and your charisma with children.”
    — Joan Chapin, Staten Island Reading Association

    “You were repeatedly identified as the highlight of the year. Your warm, personable presentations enriched the lives of all who had the opportunity to hear you. You are to be commended and applauded.”
    — Richard Osner, District Superintendent, Wurzburg District, Germany

    “What an outstanding Young Authors’ Conference! You did such an extraordinary job of speaking to our authors of all ages. You held them enthralled with the background on your books and your personal travels. Ann, you were so personal and flexible. Combine those qualities with your expertise and you have a speaker who will be hard to top.”
    — Joanne Henderson, South Carolina Young Authors Conference

    “Your presentation was lively, your enthusiasm for books contagious, your interaction with children superb! Best of all was your extraordinary capacity to captivate and hold the attention of children from grades K through 8.”
    — Principal, PS 101, New York City
