    My Family

    Click on the pictures to enlarge.

    Celebrating Marty’s 60th birthday with our four children, Charlie, Annie, Peter, and Jim and son-in-law, John

    Today there are 13 people in my family. My wonderful husband, Marty Scheiner, died in 1992. Marty had three children — Charlie, Annie and Jim. My son is Peter. Marty and I adopted each other’s children so I have four legal children and they all have spouses. I love them all. Each one is so different from the others, but we all get along splendidly.

     

    Annie and John

     

    My daughter’s name is also Ann. (We are both called Annie.) She and John have three grown children, Sharon, Chris and Dennis. Annie and John live in Maryland, close to Washington DC. Annie is a singer and voice teacher and John is a musician.

    I am so lucky to have grandchildren who are interesting and have special talents. Sharon teaches yoga. Chris is a physical education teacher and tennis pro. Dennis is a high-powered salesman.

    Charlie is an architect in New York City. Jim lives in California and is a computer specialist. My son, Peter, a freelance illustrator, lives in New York City. I love having them all close to me.

    When we all get together for holidays, the house is full of laughter and good food and good conversation. And even though our family has changed over the years, we are still all deeply connected and full of joy when we are together.
