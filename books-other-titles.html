    Other Titles

    AESOP’S FABLES

    Haven’t you heard people talk about sour grapes or not counting chickens before they hatch? Or the boy who cried wolf? Aesop’s Fables have been delighting readers for centuries.

    SHAKESPEAREAN SALLIES, SULLIES, AND SLANDERS: Insults For All Occasions

    A delightful collection of insults, putdowns, and zingers drawn from Shakespeare’s plays. Perfect for anyone who wants to argue with style and wit.

    ROBIN HOOD OF SHERWOOD FOREST

    The legendary story of Robin Hood, the champion of the poor who stole from the rich to give to the needy. Retold in Ann’s vivid, accessible style for young readers.

    HALF A KINGDOM: An Icelandic Folktale

    An ancient Icelandic tale about a brave princess who disguises herself as a boy to rescue a prince. A wonderful story of courage and ingenuity.

    SCRAM, KID!

    A young boy doesn’t want to be bothered with his little sister. But when she disappears, he realizes just how much she means to him. A touching story about sibling love.

    FEELING MAD, FEELING SAD, FEELING BAD, FEELING GLAD

    A simple, honest exploration of the full range of children’s emotions, told with warmth and illustrated with charm.

    BLACK IS BEAUTIFUL

    A lyrical celebration of the color black and all the beautiful things that are black in nature and in life. A pioneering book from 1969 that remains relevant today.
